# Interview P02: Marco

Role: operations lead and workspace admin, 40-person design agency. Team plan, 2 months in.
Interviewer: Bill. Video call, 41 minutes. Lightly cleaned transcript.

[01:50] **Bill:** Why did the agency pick Spark?

[01:58] **Marco:** Honestly, the integrations step is why we bought it. In the trial I connected Slack and Drive in the first five minutes and the whole team's files were just there. Every other tool we trialled made that a week-long IT ticket.

[03:30] **Bill:** How did onboarding go for the rest of the team?

[03:36] **Marco:** Smoother for them because I'd done the connecting. They mostly clicked through. A couple of people asked me how long setup takes, because it doesn't tell you how far along you are.

[06:05] **Bill:** Anything you'd change?

[06:09] **Marco:** I'd want admins to be able to make the integrations step required for new people. Right now anyone can skip it, and then their files don't sync and they come to me.

[09:20] **Bill:** How about after setup?

[09:25] **Marco:** Day-to-day it's solid. Permissions are a bit coarse. I can't give a freelancer access to one folder without giving them the whole project.

[14:02] **Bill:** Anything on pricing or billing?

[14:06] **Marco:** No complaints. Seat pricing is clear for us.
