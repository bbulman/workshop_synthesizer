# Interview P03: Priya

Role: independent product designer, solo account. Signed up 10 days ago, on the free trial.
Interviewer: Bill. Remote, 28 minutes. Lightly cleaned transcript.

[00:55] **Bill:** Tell me about signing up.

[01:02] **Priya:** It was quick until the connect-your-tools part. I don't want to hand over my Google Drive on day one to an app I'm still deciding about. That felt like a lot to ask.

[02:20] **Bill:** Did you connect anything?

[02:24] **Priya:** No. I skipped it, and then a few screens later it asked again. I counted, it asked me three times. That's the moment I started to think the product wasn't really for freelancers.

[05:10] **Bill:** How long did setup feel?

[05:13] **Priya:** It wasn't long exactly. It was more that I couldn't tell where the end was.

[08:45] **Bill:** Anything else that got in the way?

[08:50] **Priya:** The pricing page. I still don't know if the trial turns into a paid plan automatically. It says "14 days of Pro" and then nothing about what happens on day 15. I set a reminder on my phone so I don't get charged.

[12:30] **Bill:** Would you keep using it?

[12:34] **Priya:** Probably, for client handoffs. The share links are the best I've used.
