# Interview P04: Dana

Role: IT manager and workspace admin, 120-person nonprofit. Team plan, rolled out last month.
Interviewer: Bill. Video call, 36 minutes. Lightly cleaned transcript.

[01:15] **Bill:** What did rollout look like?

[01:20] **Dana:** I set up SSO and connected Google Workspace and Slack during onboarding. That part was good. It asked the right questions in the right order for someone like me.

[04:00] **Bill:** And for the staff?

[04:04] **Dana:** The trouble was after onboarding. People land on an empty dashboard with a big "Create your first project" button, but in our setup projects get created by team leads, not by staff. So they stare at a blank screen and assume something's broken.

[05:30] **Bill:** How did you handle that?

[05:34] **Dana:** I wrote a one-page guide. I shouldn't have had to.

[08:12] **Bill:** Did anyone mention the onboarding length?

[08:16] **Dana:** Not the length. A few people said they didn't know when it would end. It just keeps going until it doesn't.

[11:40] **Bill:** Anything you'd ask us for?

[11:44] **Dana:** An admin view of who's finished setup and who hasn't.
