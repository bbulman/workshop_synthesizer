# Interview P01: Jane

Role: freelance copywriter, solo account. Signed up 3 weeks ago.
Interviewer: Bill. Remote, 32 minutes. Lightly cleaned transcript.

[02:10] **Bill:** Walk me through the first day with Spark.

[02:18] **Jane:** I signed up on my phone, which was maybe a mistake. The first screen was fine, name and what kind of work you do. Then it wanted me to connect things. Slack, Google Drive, Notion, a calendar. I don't use Slack. I'm one person. Who am I going to message?

[03:05] **Bill:** What did you do at that point?

[03:09] **Jane:** I looked for a skip button for, honestly, longer than I should have. It's grey text at the bottom. I almost closed the tab. The integrations step felt like it was built for a company, not for me.

[04:40] **Bill:** Did you finish onboarding?

[04:44] **Jane:** Eventually. But I didn't know how many steps were left. There's no "step 3 of 5" or anything, so every time I hit next I thought, okay, surely this is the last one.

[07:12] **Bill:** What happened after onboarding?

[07:15] **Jane:** The dashboard is nice once it has stuff in it. I imported an old project and it looked great. I'd tell a friend about it, just warn them about the setup part.

[11:30] **Bill:** Anything you expected that wasn't there?

[11:34] **Jane:** A template for client briefs. I write the same brief every week.
